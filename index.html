How it works

Five simple steps. These are real screenshots of the app you get.

1

Paste your CV and the job post

Add your CV text and the text of the job you want, plus the role and company name. Not sure how it looks yet? Press Load sample data to fill it in for you. Then press Analyze my application.

2

See how well you match

You get a match score, the words you and the job post share, and the gaps to check. It compares text only, so it is a guide and not a promise of an interview.

3

Improve your CV and get a cover letter

Open the CV improvements tab for a checklist that makes your experience easier to recognize. Open Cover letter for a draft you can edit and download. Only add skills you really have.

4

Practice and follow a plan

The Interview prep tab gives you questions to practice with real examples. The Action plan tab lists what to do next. The AI prompts tab has ready prompts you can paste into your own ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.

5

Track every application

Pick a status, set a follow-up date and add notes, then press Save application locally. Your applications stay in your own browser, and you can export them as a file any time.
